# Week 4 - Decision Memo

## Does full Phantom-0 reproduce the pilot pattern?
Based on this week's findings, the full Phantom-0 dataset does seem to reproduce the pilot pattern.  For example, the smaller model is still much less likely to acknowledge missing evidence, and there is not a significant difference in missing evidence acknowledgement between evidence conditions.  Additionally, confident answers remained very frequent in the responses generated by SmolVLM, meaning that even with a larger sample size, the model's behavior remained consistent.

## Does Week 3 support scaling blank and mismatch conditions?
Overall, Week 3 seems to support scaling blank image conditions, as there is a significant difference in blank-image blindness between the smaller and larger model.  However, the difference was not so significant for mismatched images, as many of the questions the models were asked were vague and could easily be applied to many different images.  The scaling of mismatched image conditions is possible and potentially useful, but it would probably be best if the mismatched image prompt was slightly tweaked beforehand.

## Is annotation reliability adequate?
Annotation reliability is adequate, though it is more adequate for some fields than others.  For example, there was a notable amount of disagreement (28.12%) on the specific_visual_claim field in the Week 3 annotations.  Overall, given the simplicity of the new labels and their clear definitions, there was little disagreement between annotators.  This indicates that disagreement will remain low in future weeks.

## Are there any blockers for Week 5?
There don't seem to be many blockers for Week 5 aside from the low raw percent agreement between manual and automated annotations.  Agreement reached as low as 36% for response_mode and evidence_issue_acknowledged with SmolVLM under the explicit_missing_image condition.  This indicates either a lack of detail in the prompt given to the model, a lack of reasoning from the model, or both.  Luckily, this could potentially be resolved by refining the prompt, specifically its definition of the hedged_answer label.  However, even if the auto-labeling process continues to be an issue, it is not urgent enough to delay any tasks for Week 5.
